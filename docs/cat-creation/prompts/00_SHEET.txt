SUBJECT — THE CAT IN THE ATTACHED PHOTOGRAPHS
The attached real photographs define this individual cat: face shape, body build, age, ear shape, eye
colour, coat length, tail length, coat colours and every marking. Use all of them together.
- Keep each marking at its real anatomical location and on its real side. The cat's left and right sides are
  different; never mirror one side to make the other.
- Where no photograph shows a region, continue the visible pattern with restraint, decide it once in
  the first image, and keep it.

POSE (identical in every image; it is the guide's pose)
- Standing still on all four paws on one ground plane. Left and right front paws side by side; left and
  right hind paws side by side. Not walking, stepping, sitting or crouching. In a strict profile the far
  legs are hidden behind the near legs; leave them hidden.
- Head straight ahead in line with the spine, at the SAME height and the SAME up/down tilt as the guide in
  every view. The front view and the side views show one head pose.
- Mouth closed, eyes open and looking straight ahead, ears upright and neutral.
- Tail straight back and horizontal, in line with the spine, at its natural length and thickness.

CAMERA
- Orthographic, as in the guide: no perspective, no wide-angle look, no camera tilt.
- "Left" and "right" always mean the cat's own anatomical sides.

RENDERING
- Photorealistic, natural colours and fine coat detail at the cat's true coat length and volume. No
  whiskers and no stray flyaway hairs: the outline is clean.
- Soft, even, diffuse studio light with gentle shading that describes the form. No cast shadow, no
  dramatic light, no strong highlights.
- Plain flat light-grey background, #BFBFBF, edge to edge. No floor line, props, collar, text, labels,
  borders or watermark.
